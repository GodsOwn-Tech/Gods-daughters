A simple prayer guide

The ACTS model — Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication.

A

Adoration

Start by simply telling God who He is — His goodness, His power, His faithfulness. Not what He's done for you yet, just who He is.

C

Confession

Be honest about where you've fallen short — a habit, a thought, a way you treated someone. Confession isn't about shame, it's about coming clean.

T

Thanksgiving

Name a few specific things you're grateful for today — big or small. Gratitude shifts your focus even on hard days.

S

Supplication

Bring your actual requests — for yourself, for people you love, for situations weighing on you. God wants to hear the specifics.
